# FACTION ART BIBLE
### Visual Reference & Prompt Expansion Guide

*Ironwright Collective * The Fey Courts * The Demonic Kingdoms * The Celestial Crusade * The Endless*

A resource for generating diverse, visually rich card art across five factions. Each section breaks the archetype into sub-factions, character types, environments, moods, color palettes, textures, and specific scene ideas to push past generic fantasy tropes.

---

## How to Use This Document

This reference is designed to help an AI image generator break out of default visual cliches for each faction. Rather than always producing the same handful of images (e.g., every Ironwright card being a construct in a shipyard, every Fey card being a glowing fairy in a forest), use the categories below to mix and match elements for each new card.

### Prompt Construction Formula

**[Sub-Faction] + [Character Archetype] + [Action / Pose] + [Environment] + [Mood / Lighting] + [Key Texture or Material Detail] + [Color Palette Emphasis]**

*Example: "A Foundry Directorate hull-plate sentinel (Ironwright) bracing against a hull breach in a decompressing orbital shipyard, emergency klaxon lighting, poured concrete and cold-rolled iron with exposed rebar, palette of gunmetal gray, reactor blue, and slag orange."*

# THE IRONWRIGHT COLLECTIVE
*Industry * Endurance * Containment * Labor * Scale * Iron * Concrete*

**Art References:** Piranesi "Carceri d'invenzione" (impossible architecture, vertiginous industrial scale) + John Martin (apocalyptic landscapes, overwhelming scale).

**Color Palette:** Steel blue-gray (#6B7B8D), cold iron (#4A5568), warning orange (#E07020), reactor blue (#3B82C4), background (#1A1D23).

**NOT steampunk.** No brass, gears, steam, clockwork, Victorian aesthetics, or anything ornate. The Ironwright are brutalist, space-industrial, and utilitarian. Concrete, iron, hydraulics, rebar, void-reactors.

## Sub-Factions

### The Foundry Directorate (Order-aligned)
Centralized command. Purpose-built constructs. Geometric precision. Standardized, interchangeable, designed by committee. Every component has a serial number. Directorate-stamped hull plating. Modular architecture. Clean welds. Tight tolerances.

- **Character types:** Hull-plate sentinels, directorate inspectors, containment engineers, orbital overseers, standardized patrol constructs, void-dock administrators
- **Visual markers:** Directorate stamps/serial numbers on hull plates, geometric precision, modular joints, clean welds, regulation warning stripes, reactor-blue sensor arrays
- **Mood:** Institutional, oppressive, competent. Like being inside an enormous machine that works perfectly and does not care about you.

### The Scrap Legions (Chaos-aligned)
Self-assembled from battlefield wreckage. Patchwork. Jury-rigged. No two look alike. Improvised weapons welded from scrap. Exposed wiring. Mismatched hull panels. Function over form taken to the extreme. They are what happens when Ironwright constructs refuse to stop.

- **Character types:** Scrap-welded berserkers, jury-rigged war-crawlers, debris-cloud swarmers, rogue maintenance drones gone feral, salvage titans assembled from multiple wrecks
- **Visual markers:** Mismatched hull panels, exposed wiring, improvised welds (ugly, effective), scavenged reactor cores, battle damage worn as identity, no serial numbers
- **Mood:** Desperate, defiant, relentless. Ugly and terrifying because nothing that broken should still be moving.

## Environments
- Orbital shipyard: vacuum-exposed scaffolding, half-built hulls, magnetic cranes, zero-gravity debris
- Strip-mine canyon: terraced excavation, bucket-wheel extractors, iron-dust atmosphere, slag heaps
- Void-reactor core: cathedral-scale reactor chamber, containment rings, plasma glow, heat distortion
- Gravity-tether anchorage: massive cables descending to planetary surface, tension monitors, vertigo scale
- Decommission yard: rows of deactivated constructs, salvage crews, sparking cutting torches, rain on iron
- Foundry floor: molten iron channels, crucibles, casting molds, chain-driven overhead transport
- Bulkhead corridor: pressurized interior, emergency lighting, blast doors, coolant pipes, low ceiling
- Hull-breach aftermath: torn metal, vacuum exposure, emergency foam, floating debris, distant stars
- Command bridge: instrument panels, viewport showing industrial operations, communications arrays
- Underground silo: vertical shaft, suspended platforms, reactor fuel rods, echoing depth
- Transit rail: magnetic rail through tunnel, construct passengers, sparking overhead power lines
- Atmospheric processor: massive machinery converting atmosphere, exhaust stacks, condensation clouds
- Dry dock: ship in repair cradle, workers on scaffolding, welding arcs, scale of construction

## Moods & Palettes
- Institutional control: Flat overhead lighting, gunmetal blue-gray/cold iron/warning orange stripes/white
- Reactor emergency: Red klaxon glow, sparks, alarm red/reactor blue/smoke gray/molten slag-orange
- Void exposure: Stars through breach, vacuum cold, deep black/steel blue/frost white/distant reactor glow
- Industrial pride: Warm foundry light, freshly poured iron glow, amber/dark iron/slag orange/concrete gray
- Decommission grief: Rain, overcast, rust/weathered concrete/dead sensor lights/puddle reflections
- Oppressive scale: Tiny figures in enormous spaces, Piranesi vertigo, dark iron/distance haze/cold blue

## Textures
Poured concrete (shuttering marks visible), cold-rolled iron plate, exposed rebar (rusted, structural), hydraulic pistons (oil-slick chrome), weld beads (rough industrial, not decorative), bolted flanges, reactor containment rings, warning paint stripes (faded), vulcanized rubber seals, corrugated hull plating, magnetic-locked panels, cable bundles, coolant pipe frost, pressure gauge glass, emergency foam residue, slag (rough, glassy), iron filings, concrete dust, carbon scoring from plasma cutting. No brass. No copper. No ornamental metalwork.

# THE FEY COURTS
*Glamour * Wildness * Nature * Cycles * Timelessness*

**Art References:** Arthur Rackham (died 1939 -- PD) + Edmund Dulac (died 1953 -- PD). Twisted ink linework, watercolor washes, storybook illustration aesthetic.

**Color Palette:** Emerald green (#2D8F4E), golden amber (#C4A035), bioluminescent teal (#4ECDC4), deep bark brown (#3E2723), background (#0D1A0D).

## Sub-Factions

### The Verdant Throne (Order-aligned, Spring/Summer)
Growth, exploding flowers, pollen storms, vines cracking stone. Patient, ancient, and confident. The natural order is their order. Harmony with cycles. Controlled growth. Crystallized permanence. The still forest at peace.

- **Character types:** Thornwood wardens, grove sentinels, antlered fey lords, dryad warriors, living root serpents, starborn protectors, bioluminescent sprite scouts
- **Visual markers:** Living bark armor grown into shape (not forged), moonflower crowns, bioluminescent freckle markings, crystallized sap jewelry, moss-covered joints, root-systems as garments
- **Mood:** Serene, overwhelming, ancient. Like standing in a cathedral made of living wood that has been growing since before civilization.

### The Hollow Court (Chaos-aligned, Autumn/Winter)
Silence. Bare branches like bones. Frozen ponds with faces beneath. Moth-eaten elegance. The wild hunt. Primal fury older than gods. Untameable power. The forest in storm. Decay as transformation.

- **Character types:** Wild hunt riders, spectral hounds, frost-touched fey assassins, mushroom ring necromancers, bone orchard sentinels, moth-winged prophets, autumn harvest collectors
- **Visual markers:** Bare antlers (no velvet), frost patterns on bark, hollow eye sockets, moth-wing cloaks, dried-flower crowns, mycelial network tendrils, bioluminescence in cold blue-green
- **Mood:** Predatory, melancholy, inevitable. Like the moment before the hunt horn sounds and everything runs.

## Environments
- Ancient canopy cathedral: impossibly tall trees forming natural vaults, filtered green-gold light, motes of pollen
- Bioluminescent grotto: underground cave, glowing mushrooms and fungi, mycelial network visible, still water
- Thorn maze: 30-foot living briar walls, shifting passages, bones in the hedge, wrong turns
- Hollow tree palace: impossibly vast tree interior, spiral staircases of living wood, windows showing different seasons
- Fairy ring crossroads: mushroom circle at midnight, space inside brighter/darker than it should be
- Drowning garden: underwater flowers, flooded meadow with filtered sunlight, fish through rose bushes
- Bone orchard: trees of interlocking bones (antlers, ribs) blooming with real flowers, disturbing beauty
- Wild hunt path: moonlit forest trail, trampled undergrowth, spectral hoofprints glowing, urgency
- Frozen glade: ice-covered clearing, bare trees, breath visible, something watching from the treeline
- Spider-silk observatory: web structure between trees, dewdrop lenses, spider-fey astronomers, starlight
- Petrified dance floor: stone figures mid-dance, ecstasy and terror, moss creeping slowly over them
- Mirror lake: perfectly still water reflecting a different time, a different season, a different world
- Cocoon cathedral: silk cocoons of various sizes, some glowing within, moth-winged attendants in silence

## Moods & Palettes
- Enchanted allure: Soft dappled light, golden hour, emerald/gold/warm amber/cream/honey
- Creeping dread: Dim blue twilight, bioluminescence below, deep teal/bruise purple/sickly yellow-green/black
- Predatory pursuit: Moonlight through branches, motion blur, silver/cobalt blue/hunter green/blood red
- Frozen stillness: Blue-white winter light, ice blue/bone white/bare wood gray/black berries
- Overwhelming growth: Noon light through dense canopy, chlorophyll greens/soil brown/pollen yellow

## Textures
Iridescent beetle shells, spider silk (wet and dry), bark (birch paper, oak roughness, rotting), mushroom gills, lichen crust, flower petals (fresh and dried), antler velvet and bare antler bone, fox fur, moth wing dust, dragonfly wing venation, honeycomb wax, amber resin, river stones, cobweb lace, frozen dew drops, autumn leaf translucency, mycelial threads, bioluminescent fungus glow, thistledown, rose thorn hooks, pinecone spirals, acorn caps.

# THE DEMONIC KINGDOMS
*Ambition * Corruption * Hierarchy * Power * Transformation*

**Art References:** Hieronymus Bosch (died 1516 -- PD). Surreal grotesquerie, impossible anatomies, teeming detail, the mundane made nightmarish.

**Color Palette:** Hellfire red (#C41E3A), obsidian black (#1A1A2E), molten gold (#DAA520), sulfur yellow (#B8860B), background (#1A0A0A).

## Sub-Factions

### The Furnace Lords (Chaos-aligned)
Wrath. Volcanic forges. Magma rivers. Jagged obsidian armor. War banners of flame. Military aristocracy of hell -- they earned their rank through violence and hold it through fear. Raw, explosive, overwhelming power. Self-immolation as strategy.

- **Character types:** Obsidian-armored warlords, hellfire elementals, ashclaw ravagers, infernal colossals, volcanic siege beasts, flame-banner heralds, magma-blood berserkers
- **Visual markers:** Jagged obsidian plate armor, hellfire erupting from joints, war banners of living flame, molten blood visible through cracked skin, volcanic ash as warpaint, infernal runes glowing red
- **Mood:** Overwhelming, violent, magnificent. Like staring into a volcanic eruption that is staring back at you.

### The Obsidian Bureaucracy (Order-aligned)
Contracts. Loopholes. Towering filing cabinets. Stamp seals that brand skin. The real power in hell is not the monster -- it is the paperwork that controls the monster. Infernal lawyers. Soul accountants. The quiet ones are more dangerous.

- **Character types:** Contract archivists, soul brokers, infernal barristers, stamp-seal executors, filing-cabinet golems, loophole demons, chain-of-custody wardens
- **Visual markers:** Black-and-gold vestments, contract scrolls in blood ink, spectacles with too many lenses, bureaucratic stamps that leave brands, chains as both binding and jewelry, orderly despite the horror
- **Mood:** Clinical, inevitable, suffocating. The worst thing about hell is the forms you have to fill out in triplicate.

## Environments
- Volcanic court: Throne room in caldera, obsidian pillars, lava channels as moat, heat haze distortion
- Soul exchange: Trading floor for souls, price boards, frantic brokers, soul-jars in pneumatic tubes
- Infernal shipyard: Ships of bone and black iron, rivers of fire, skeletal crews, volcanic docks
- Garden of Earthly Delights: Bosch-inspired surreal landscape, giant fruit, impossible creatures teeming
- Penitent's staircase: Infinite stone stairs descending, each step carved with a different sin
- Corruption nursery: Demonic creatures grown in pods, half-formed beings, clinical observation
- Library of sins: Self-shelving books of transgressions, too-many-eyed librarians, ink that moves
- Screaming colosseum: Amphitheater for combat disputes, tiered spectators, bookmakers, almost normal
- Frozen lake of treachery: Dante's lowest circle, traitors frozen in ice, eerily silent and cold
- Obsidian bureaucracy office: Filing cabinets floor to ceiling, fluorescent lighting revealing true forms
- Blood ritual chamber: Concentric circles, candles, channels for flowing blood, something being summoned
- Infernal market: Bazaar selling body modifications, soul fragments, stolen memories, haggling demons
- Volcanic battlefield: Ash-choked sky, obsidian shards as terrain, hellfire explosions, war banner silhouettes

## Moods & Palettes
- Volcanic fury: Underlit by magma, deep shadow above, magma orange/obsidian black/sulfur yellow/blood red
- Bureaucratic dread: Harsh fluorescent, clinical, sickly yellow-green/institutional gray/manila/red stamp ink
- Seductive luxury: Warm candlelight, gold reflections, burgundy/gold/black velvet/plum/dark honey
- Grotesque beauty: Chiaroscuro Renaissance light, flesh tones/bone white/organ red/bruise spectrum
- Existential void: Absence of light, negative space, pure black/void blue/faint gray/single white point

## Textures
Cracked obsidian, cooled lava (ropy and jagged), sulfur crystals, black iron (pitted and ancient), skin (stretched, as parchment), bone (polished, raw, fossilized), horn and chitin, scales, velvet and silk in decay, molten gold, congealed blood, crystallized tears, chains (rusted, gilded, ethereal), smoke and ash, brimstone powder, wax (melting, dripping), cracked marble, tarnished silver, burnt parchment, ink that moves, cracked mirror glass, fungal growth, coral-like demonic growths.

# THE CELESTIAL CRUSADE
*Authority * Radiance * Righteousness * Crusade * Terrifying Beauty*

**Art References:** Gustave Dore biblical illustrations (died 1883 -- PD) + William Blake visionary paintings (died 1827 -- PD). Overwhelming divine scale, geometric impossibility, light as substance, the terrifying beauty of the divine.

**Color Palette:** Holy gold (#DAA520), divine ivory (#F5F0E1), righteous blue (#3B5998), celestial rose (#C47A8E), background (#1A1520).

## Sub-Factions

### Knights of Deliverance (Order-aligned)
Stoic paladins. Divine armor. Formation discipline. The military arm. Gold-plated heavy infantry. White marble skin. Moving in perfect geometric formations. They do not doubt. They do not negotiate. They deliver.

- **Character types:** Radiant squires, consecrated knights, formation crusaders, holy marshals, divine lancers, prayer-ward sentinels, bastion paladins, judgment executors
- **Visual markers:** Hammered gold plate armor, white tabards with divine sun emblem, faintly visible golden halos, divine blue silk under armor, formation markers (geometric positioning), marble-white skin veined with gold, cathedral light following them
- **Mood:** Absolute, terrifying, beautiful. Like watching an army of saints marching in perfect formation and knowing they will not stop for anything.

### Heaven's Chosen (Chaos-aligned)
Biblically-accurate angels. Multi-winged, multi-eyed celestials. Sacred geometry made flesh. Burning wheels (ophanim). Seraphim with six wings. The divine arm. They are what the Celestials pray to. Meeting one changes you permanently. Not evil -- just incomprehensible.

- **Character types:** Burning wheel manifestations, seraphim with six wings (two covering face, two covering feet, two in flight), many-eyed watchers, sacred geometry entities (impossible shapes of light), divine heralds (trumpets that crack reality), apostles wreathed in fire
- **Visual markers:** Concentric rings of eyes, multiple sets of wings (feathered AND burning), sacred geometry patterns in the air around them, light bending incorrectly, stained glass effect in the air, rose windows as body parts
- **Mood:** Incomprehensible, overwhelming, ecstatic. "Be not afraid" but you are, desperately, afraid.

## Environments
- Radiant Bastion: Fortress of white marble and gold, light emanating from within the walls, no shadows
- Cloud battlefield: Above the clouds, golden light, divine formations clashing, feathers and light falling
- Sanctum of Open Eyes: Cathedral interior, every surface covered in painted eyes, incense smoke, golden dust motes
- Consecration site: Ordinary landscape transformed by divine presence, grass turning gold, stones singing
- Burning bridge between realms: Bridge of solidified light spanning void, celestial entities crossing, vertigo
- Judgment hall: Enormous scales of gold, books of deeds, divine light from above, no escape
- Rose window cathedral: Stained glass walls floor to ceiling, light creating colored patterns on marble floors
- Divine armory: Racks of luminous weapons, each one unique, angels selecting for the next crusade
- Heaven's threshold: Where the mortal world meets the divine, reality fraying at the edges, blinding light
- Formation grounds: Geometric grid of golden light, knights training in perfect synchronization
- Choir chamber: Tiers of singing celestials, sound visible as golden waves, walls vibrating
- Pilgrimage road: Long road through varied terrain, pilgrims in white, destination glowing on horizon
- Apotheosis chamber: Where mortals become divine, light pouring into a figure, transformation mid-process

## Moods & Palettes
- Divine authority: Cathedral light from above, holy gold/divine ivory/righteous blue/marble white
- Terrifying beauty: Overwhelming brightness, lens flare, gold/blinding white/celestial rose/light consuming edges
- Righteous fury: Golden fire, judgment light, burning gold/divine blue/crimson accent/incandescent white
- Geometric perfection: Symmetrical light, sacred geometry, gold/ivory/blue/rose in precise patterns
- Incomprehensible divinity: Light bending wrong, colors that don't exist, iridescent white-gold/void edges

## Textures
Hammered gold plate (dented by purpose, not wear), white marble veined with gold, divine blue silk (impossibly smooth), solidified light (translucent, warm to look at), stained glass (casting colored shadows), feathers (white, gold-tipped, burning), sacred geometry inscriptions, incense smoke, golden dust motes, rose petals preserved in light, crystal (clear, singing), holy water (luminous), divine script (glowing, hovering), chains of prayer (golden, light), consecrated stone (warm to the touch), illuminated manuscript gold leaf.

# THE ENDLESS
*Persistence * Death * Accumulation * Memory * The Refusal to End*

**Art References:** Gustave Dore Inferno etchings (died 1883 -- PD) + Francisco Goya "Black Paintings" (died 1828 -- PD). Heavy shadow, grotesque dignity, the weight of death as an industry, grief as a force of nature.

**Color Palette:** Necrotic purple (#6B3FA0), bone white (#E8DCC8), ghostly teal (#5F9EA0), sickly green (#7B9E5F), background (#0D0D1A).

## Sub-Factions

### Necromantic Cabals (Order-aligned)
Liches raising armies. Bone constructs. Phylacteries. The power brokers. Academic, deliberate, patient. Death as an engineering problem. Reanimation as an industry. They have had centuries to refine their methods and they are very, very good at this. Dry wit. Mild amusement at the living.

- **Character types:** Cabal necromancers (scholarly robes, phylactery pendants), bone-stitched soldiers (mass-produced, standardized), ossuary wardens (guardians of the bone stores), phylactery guardians (protecting soul-containers), lich architects (designing death-infrastructure), bone colossals (cathedral-scale bone constructs)
- **Visual markers:** Polished bone (not rough -- industrially processed), phylactery glow (teal-green), necrotic purple runes carved into bone, academic robes (tattered but dignified), spectacles on skull faces, soul-containers (glass jars, bone boxes), standardized construction (modular bone plates)
- **Mood:** Academic, methodical, unsettling. Like visiting a factory where the product is death and the quality control is excellent.

### The Lost Spectres (Chaos-aligned)
Ghosts and spectres abandoned by the liches. Ethereal, tragic, furious. They remember who they were. They did not ask for this. Grief and rage given spectral form. Wild, uncontrolled, empathetic in a way the Cabals are not. They haunt because they cannot stop.

- **Character types:** Wailing remnants (grief-spectres), spectral hunters (translucent predators), ghost swarms (many small spectres moving as one), forgotten champions (spectral warriors from old wars), memory echoes (replaying their death forever), spectral maelstroms (uncontrolled death energy)
- **Visual markers:** Translucent/flickering between solid and ghost, tattered burial shrouds, ectoplasmic trails, frozen grief expressions, spectral chains (binding them to this world), fading edges (dissolving at extremities), ghostly teal glow from within
- **Mood:** Mournful, fierce, haunting. Like hearing someone weeping in an empty room and realizing it has been going on for centuries and will not stop.

## Environments
- Ossuary Parliament: Governing chamber built entirely of bones, bone throne, bone voting booths, dim teal glow
- Wailing Reach: Fog-choked cliff edge, spectres drifting over void, distant mournful sounds, vertigo
- Necromancer's theater: Amphitheater for reanimation demonstrations, bone-tier seating, academic audience
- Underground crypt: Endless tunnels of stacked bones, phylactery alcoves, dripping water, dust motes in teal light
- Spectral battlefield: Ancient battlefield, ghostly replays of old combat, transparent armies clashing
- Bone workshop: Industrial bone processing, sorting, polishing, assembly, quality control, clinical lighting
- Wailing wall: Stone wall where spectres are trapped, faces pressing through stone, ectoplasmic residue
- Graveyard at dusk: Tilted headstones, fog rolling in, spectral figures emerging, lanterns flickering
- Lich's study: Book-lined walls, skull collections, correspondence with other liches, academic clutter
- Ghost ship: Spectral vessel on dark water, transparent crew, sails of ectoplasm, destination unknown
- Memory garden: Space where dead memories manifest as ghostly flowers, each one a life remembered
- Bone cathedral: Cathedral built entirely of fused bones, stained glass of preserved spectral energy
- Soul forge: Where phylacteries are made, soul-energy being processed, clinical and industrial

## Moods & Palettes
- Academic necromancy: Cool even lighting, institutional, bone white/necrotic purple/teal accents/parchment
- Spectral grief: Fog-diffused moonlight, ghostly teal/bone white/fading edges/ectoplasmic green
- Death industry: Clinical overhead lighting, bone white/institutional gray/phylactery teal/necrotic purple
- Haunted battlefield: Twilight, mist rising, ghostly replay, muted gray/spectral teal/blood brown/silence

## Textures
Bone (polished industrial, raw archaeological, fused architectural), ectoplasm (translucent, viscous, glowing), tattered cloth (burial shrouds, academic robes, moth-eaten), spectral energy (teal glow, flickering, unstable), phylactery crystal (dark glass, soul-light within), necrotic runes (carved into bone, glowing purple), preserved flesh (leathered, dried, academic specimens), dust (bone dust, grave dust, accumulated centuries), cobwebs (thick, old, structural), rusted iron (coffin nails, cemetery gates), fungal growth (on bone, slow, persistent), frozen tears, candle wax (ancient, layered), parchment (yellowed, brittle, covered in necrotic script).

# PLANAR RUINS
*Ancient * Neutral * Mysterious * Transformable * Powerful*

Planar Ruins are a card type representing ancient structures left by the Ancient Builders. They start as faction-neutral and can be evolved into faction-specific variants using img2img transformation. The neutral base art must be evocative but deliberately faction-agnostic.

**Art References:** Piranesi "Carceri" (impossible architecture) + Gustave Dore (overwhelming scale) + John Martin (apocalyptic landscapes). The ruins should feel ANCIENT -- older than any living faction, built by something that understood reality differently.

**Color Palette (neutral):** Warm sandstone (#C4A882), void-dark (#1A1A2E), energy blue-white (#B8D4E3), moss green (#6B8E5A), weathered bronze (#8B7355).

## The 8 Neutral Ruin Archetypes

1. **Resonance Spire:** Tall crystalline structure that hums with contained energy. Geometric precision. Cracks glowing with ancient power. Standing alone in a barren landscape.
2. **Anchor Plinth:** Low, broad stone platform with deep-carved channels. Something was chained here. The chains are gone. The anchor points remain. Weight of purpose.
3. **Mote Well:** Circular stone well with energy rising from it like reverse rain. Motes of light ascending. Ancient carved lip. Depth implied, not shown.
4. **Sight Glass:** Large curved crystalline lens mounted in a stone frame. When you look through it, the world looks different. Cracks in the lens. Ancient mounting hardware.
5. **War Cairn:** Deliberately stacked stones marking a boundary or warning. Carved with symbols no one can read. Moss-covered. Birds avoid it.
6. **Threshold Gate:** Two standing stones with a lintel. The space between them is slightly wrong -- the light bends, the air shimmers. Something used to pass through here.
7. **Communion Altar:** Flat stone surface with channels and basins. Purpose unknown but clearly ritual. Offerings would go here. Something would answer.
8. **Oblivion Obelisk:** Tall, black, featureless stone that absorbs light. No carvings -- or the carvings are on the inside. Standing in a clearing where nothing grows.

## Faction Evolution Visual Transformations

When a neutral ruin is claimed by a faction, the img2img transformation should show the faction's aesthetic GROWING INTO the ruin's structure, not replacing it. The ancient bones of the structure remain visible under the faction overlay.

- **Ironwright transformation:** Concrete poured into cracks, rebar reinforcement bolted onto ancient stone, industrial scaffolding around the structure, reactor power cables plugged into ancient energy channels, warning paint stripes, Directorate claim markers.
- **Fey Courts transformation:** Living wood and vines growing through and around the stone, bioluminescent fungi colonizing surfaces, roots cracking into the structure's channels, flowers blooming from cracks, moss covering ancient carvings, the ruin becoming part of the forest.
- **Demonic Kingdoms transformation:** Obsidian growth like crystal formations on stone surfaces, hellfire channeled through ancient carved lines, blood-red sigils overlaid on original carvings, infernal chains binding the structure, volcanic heat distortion, cracks glowing with molten energy.
- **Celestial Crusade transformation:** Gold leaf and divine inscriptions applied over ancient carvings, stained glass installed in openings, divine light emanating from within, white marble additions buttressing ancient stone, consecration marks, incense smoke rising, the ruin becoming a shrine.
- **Endless transformation:** Bone growth fusing with ancient stone, spectral energy pooling in carved channels, necrotic purple runes overlaid on original carvings, ectoplasmic residue in the cracks, phylacteries mounted in alcoves, the ruin becoming a mausoleum.

# CROSS-FACTION VARIETY STRATEGIES

1. **Rotate sub-factions:** Never two consecutive cards from the same sub-faction
2. **Vary scale:** Alternate close-ups, medium shots, epic wide shots
3. **Vary time of day and weather:** Unexpected combos (Ironwright at dawn, Fey at harsh noon, Demonic in snowfall, Celestial at dusk, Endless at high noon)
4. **Include non-combat moments:** Eating, mourning, celebrating, teaching, building, trading, scheming, studying, praying, remembering
5. **Show faction intersections:** Ironwright engineer reverse-engineering ruin technology, demon diplomat at celestial border, endless spectre haunting a fey grove
6. **Age and body diversity:** Elderly masters, apprentices, scarred veterans, varied body types, ancient liches, young squires
7. **"What If" expander:** Add rain, unusual angle, something gone wrong, character's first day, unusual sub-faction environment, unexpected emotional moment
8. **Ruins as anchor:** Place a Planar Ruin in the background of faction cards -- shows the ancient world beneath the current one
