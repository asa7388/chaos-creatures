Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 17, 2026

Chaos Creatures ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is a collectible card game for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Apple, we receive:

  • Email address - Your Apple ID email (or a private relay address if you choose to hide your email). Used for account identification and support communication.
  • User ID - A unique identifier assigned to your account. Used to associate your game data with your account.

Gameplay Data

We collect data related to your gameplay to provide the game experience:

  • Card collection and deck configurations
  • Match history and results
  • Player level, experience points, and in-game currency balances
  • Quest and achievement progress
  • Faction selection and avatar choice

Purchase History

When you make in-app purchases, Apple processes all payment information. We receive a record of what was purchased (subscription tier or in-game item) but never receive your payment card details, billing address, or other financial information.

Analytics Data

We collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the game:

  • App launch and session duration
  • Screen views and feature usage
  • Match queue times and completion rates
  • Crash reports and error logs

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account management - To create and maintain your game account, save your progress, and sync your data across sessions.
  • Gameplay - To operate the game, including matchmaking, maintaining leaderboards, processing in-game transactions, and delivering game content.
  • Analytics - To understand how players use the game so we can fix bugs, improve performance, and make the game more enjoyable.
  • Support - To respond to your support requests and communicate important updates about the game.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the App:

Supabase (Authentication and Database)

Your account information and gameplay data are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase on AWS infrastructure. Supabase provides authentication services for Sign in with Apple. Supabase Privacy Policy

PostHog (Analytics)

We use PostHog to collect anonymous usage analytics. PostHog helps us understand gameplay patterns and improve the App. Analytics data is not used for advertising. PostHog Privacy Policy

Apple (Payments and App Distribution)

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles all payment processing. Apple Privacy Policy

4. AI-Generated Content

All card art in Chaos Creatures is generated using artificial intelligence (fal.ai FLUX) and card text is generated using OpenAI. This content generation happens entirely on our servers. No user data, images, or personal information is sent to these AI services. The AI is used solely to create game content (card illustrations and flavor text) based on game design parameters.

5. Advertising and Tracking

Chaos Creatures does not display advertisements. We do not use any advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks. We do not sell, share, or provide your data to advertisers or data brokers. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data and gameplay information for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, we will delete your personal data within 30 days. Anonymous, aggregated analytics data that cannot be linked to you may be retained indefinitely.

7. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (TLS/SSL), secure authentication tokens, and row-level security policies on our database. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following rights:

  • Access - You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction - You can request that we correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion - You can request that we delete your account and personal data. Use the "Delete Account" feature in the App's settings, or contact us at the email below.
  • Portability - You can request your data in a portable format.
  • Objection - You can object to certain processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@chaoscreatures.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" of personal information. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise your rights, contact us at support@chaoscreatures.app.

10. Children's Privacy

Chaos Creatures is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@chaoscreatures.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the App or by updating the "Last updated" date above. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
