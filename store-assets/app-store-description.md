## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Every card tells a story. Yours.

Chaos Creatures is a collectible card game where every card's art is generated by AI and evolves through play. No two collections look alike. The cards you build, the battles you fight, and the choices you make at each evolution shape a deck that is entirely your own.

ROLL THE D20. EMBRACE THE CHAOS.

At the start of every turn, roll a D20 Chaos Roll. The result triggers Order events, Chaos events, or something in between — depending on your deck's instability rating. Build toward Order for consistency, lean into Chaos for explosive upside, or ride the edge between both.

FIVE FACTIONS. FIVE PHILOSOPHIES.

- The Ironwright Collective: Brutalist war machines forged in orbital shipyards. Stack Augments on a single creature to create an unstoppable siege engine.
- The Fey Courts: Ancient forests and bioluminescent fey lords. Bond your creatures together so the whole board fights as one.
- The Demonic Kingdoms: Hellfire and blood rituals. Corrupt your own creatures, trading health for devastating power.
- The Celestial Crusade: Divine crusaders and multi-winged celestials. Exalt your formation to empower every creature on the field.
- The Endless: Liches, spectres, and the relentless undead. Every creature you kill triggers Persist effects that haunt you.

STRATEGIC COMBAT

Declare attackers. Assign blockers. Every battle is a tactical puzzle where positioning and timing matter. Seven keywords — Shield, Lifesteal, Flying, Reach, Deathtouch, Taunt, Piercing — create deep interactions that reward smart deckbuilding.

EVOLVE YOUR CARDS

Play games. Earn chaos energy. When a card is ready, channel it through a Planar Shard and watch it transform.

Every evolution gives your card new stats, a new ability, a new modifier, and brand-new AI-generated art that builds on its previous form. A Common you found on day one can become a Legendary with a visual history written across four transformations. Two players who evolved the same base card will have different Legendaries — because every evolution path diverges.

Common. Uncommon. Rare. Epic. Legendary. Every rarity is earned, never bought.

NO PAY-TO-WIN

All cards are earned through gameplay using Chaos Dust, the in-game currency. There are no card packs for real money and no power-locked content behind a paywall. Optional subscriptions enhance your evolution experience — more modifier choices, more collection space — but a free player with skill will always beat a paying player.

PLAY YOUR WAY

- Practice Mode: Hone your skills against AI opponents
- Ranked PvP: Compete in real-time matches against other players
- Seasonal Events: New content, challenges, and rewards each season

FEATURES

- AI-generated card art that evolves through image-to-image transformation
- D20 Chaos Roll with Order and Chaos events every turn
- 5 factions with exclusive mechanics (Augment, Bond, Corruption, Exalt, Persist)
- MTG-style tactical combat with attackers and blockers
- 5-tier card evolution from Common to Legendary
- 7 keywords for deep strategic deckbuilding
- Real-time PvP matchmaking
- Practice mode against AI bots
- Seasonal content and battle passes
- No pay-to-win: all cards earnable through gameplay
