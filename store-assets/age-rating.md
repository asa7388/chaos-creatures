# Age Rating Questionnaire — Chaos Creatures

Apple's age rating questionnaire in App Store Connect. These answers determine the content rating shown on the App Store.

## Questionnaire Answers

| Category | Answer | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Cartoon or Fantasy Violence | **Infrequent/Mild** | Card battles involve fantasy creatures attacking each other. Damage is shown as floating numbers. Creature death is a fade-out or shatter animation. No blood, no gore, no realistic violence. |
| Realistic Violence | **None** | All combat is stylized card game combat between fantasy creatures. No depiction of realistic violence against people or animals. |
| Sexual Content or Nudity | **None** | No sexual content. Card art depicts fantasy creatures, machines, and environments. No nudity. |
| Profanity or Crude Humor | **None** | All text is AI-generated and reviewed. Flavor text uses fantasy language. No profanity, crude humor, or offensive language. |
| Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References | **None** | No references to alcohol, tobacco, or drugs in card art, flavor text, or game mechanics. |
| Simulated Gambling | **None** | Card packs are purchased with Chaos Dust, an in-game currency earned through gameplay only. No real-money randomized loot boxes. The D20 Chaos Roll is a gameplay mechanic (like rolling dice in a board game), not a gambling mechanic. No wagering of any kind. |
| Horror/Fear Themes | **Infrequent/Mild** | The Demonic Kingdoms faction includes fantasy horror imagery: hellfire, obsidian fortresses, corrupted creatures. Art style is painterly fantasy (not photorealistic horror). Comparable to fantasy card games rated 9+. |
| Medical/Treatment Information | **None** | No medical content. |
| Contests | **None** | No real-money contests or sweepstakes. PvP matchmaking is for in-game progression only. |
| Unrestricted Web Access | **None** | The app does not include a web browser. All external links (privacy policy, terms of service) open in Safari via standard iOS URL handling. |

## Expected Rating: 9+

The combination of Infrequent/Mild fantasy violence and Infrequent/Mild horror themes (Demonic faction artwork) produces a 9+ rating. This is consistent with comparable card games on the App Store (Hearthstone: 9+, Marvel Snap: 9+, Magic: The Gathering Arena: 12+).

## Notes for App Review

- The D20 Chaos Roll is a core gameplay mechanic (dice-based event trigger), not simulated gambling. No real money is wagered, and the outcome affects turn events, not card acquisition.
- Card packs are purchased exclusively with Chaos Dust, which cannot be bought with real money. This does not qualify as simulated gambling under Apple's guidelines.
- Subscriptions enhance evolution modifier selection breadth (cosmetic/convenience) and never grant gameplay power advantages.
- AI-generated card art is reviewed for quality and appropriateness before inclusion in the game.
